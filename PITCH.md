## 1. Public Presence Audit (Verified 12 Nov 2026)

1. **Zero Working Website:**
   - **CAMRA WhatPub / directory listing** explicitly states *"No website available"*.
   - Legacy `osbournes.co.uk/yarm` URLs fail with **broken/dead TLS errors** — anyone clicking old links hits a browser security warning instead of a menu or booking page.
2. **Conflicting Public Phone Numbers Across Yarm Directories:**
   - **This Is Yarm** lists `01642 781012`, while **CAMRA** lists `01642 801012`.
   - Because there is no authoritative official website, Google and visitors searching *"Osbournes Yarm brunch"* or *"book table Osbournes Yarm"* see contradictory numbers and no confirmed kitchen hours. *(In this concept demo, we deliberately display `[Phone to confirm]` with zero guessed `tel:` link so we never publish the wrong line.)*
3. **Clean UK Domain Ready to Claim Immediately:**
   - **`osbournesyarm.co.uk`** was verified **unregistered on Nominet on 12 Nov 2026** — Osbournes can own their dedicated, independent Yarm domain outright for a few pounds a year without relying on any broken group sub-folder.
4. **High-Margin Daytime + Weekend Revenue Leakage:**
   - Yarm High Street brunch tables, matchday groups, and Friday/Saturday birthday booth enquiries currently depend on people scrolling Facebook (`OsbournesYarm`) or Instagram (`@osbournes.yarm`) — locking out non-social searchers and making menu checking awkward on phones.
